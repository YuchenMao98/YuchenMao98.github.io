Research

Research interests

My research lies at the intersection of operations research and statistics, with an emphasis on data-driven decision-making under uncertainty and its applications in business operations.

  • Optimization under uncertainty: stochastic programming, robust optimization, and distributionally robust optimization.
  • Econometrics and forecasting: causal inference, time-series models, and predictive decision frameworks.
  • Business applications: supply-chain analytics, production planning, and service operations.
