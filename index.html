About me

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Statistics at Rutgers University–New Brunswick, advised by Prof. Yifan Hu.

My research lies at the intersection of operations research and statistics. I am interested in data-driven decision-making under uncertainty, econometrics, and applications in supply-chain and service operations.

Before joining Rutgers, I was a research associate and received my M.Sc. in Management Science from the University of the Chinese Academy of Sciences. I received my B.Sc. in Statistics from Central China Normal University.
