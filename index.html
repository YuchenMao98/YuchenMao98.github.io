About

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Statistics at Rutgers University–New Brunswick, advised by Prof. Yifan Hu.

Before joining Rutgers, I worked as a research associate and earned my M.Sc. in Management Science at the University of the Chinese Academy of Sciences (2020–2025), under the supervision of Prof. Shuming Wang. I received my B.Sc. in Statistics from Central China Normal University in 2020.
